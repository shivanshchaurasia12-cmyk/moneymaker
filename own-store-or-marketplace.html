Own Store or Marketplace? A Decision Guide for Small Sellers in India

By the Money Moves India editorial team · Published 1 October 2026 · 4 min read

"Which is better, Shopify or Meesho?" is the wrong question. They solve different problems. A marketplace gives you shoppers who are already there. An own store gives you control, but you have to bring the shoppers yourself.

The main differences

TopicMarketplaceOwn store
CustomersAlready browsing the platformYou must bring every visitor
Cost per saleCommission and fees taken from each orderPayment fee plus your advertising cost
Fixed costUsually none, or lowMonthly platform plan and apps
BrandingLimited. Your product sits next to rivals.Full control of look, emails and customer list
RulesPlatform decides on pricing pressure, returns and penaltiesYou decide, within the law
Customer dataMostly held by the platformYou hold the data and can follow up

A worked example

Imagine a handmade product that sells for Rs 499 and costs Rs 220 to make. Shipping is Rs 60. The fee figures below are made up for the example. Replace them with each platform's current numbers.

MarketplaceOwn store
Selling priceRs 499Rs 499
Product costRs 220Rs 220
ShippingRs 60Rs 60
Platform fees (example)Rs 75Rs 10 payment fee
Advertising per orderRs 0Rs 120
Left per orderRs 144Rs 89

On the first sale, the marketplace wins by Rs 55, and the own store also has a monthly plan cost on top. So why would anyone build their own store?

Because of repeat customers. If an average buyer orders 1.5 times, your advertising cost per order falls from Rs 120 to Rs 80, and the second order costs almost nothing to win if you can email or message the customer. A marketplace does not usually let you do that. The own store does not win on margin at the start. It wins when your customers come back.

Which one fits you?

  • Start on a marketplace if you are testing a product, have little money for ads, or want your first sales quickly.
  • Add an own store once you have a product that sells and customers who ask for you by name.
  • Do not pay for a store plan and ads before you know a single product can make money.

Before you choose

Ask each platform about the seller account rules, the return and cancellation policy, how long payouts take, and what happens if a customer disputes an order. If a platform requires an adult account holder, use a parent or guardian's genuine details. Never use a false age or someone else's identity.

Costs people forget

  • Returns. On some marketplaces you pay for the return shipping and you may not be able to resell the item. Read the return policy for your product type first.
  • Price pressure. On a marketplace, a rival can list the same item cheaper than you in minutes. An own store is not exposed in the same way, but then nobody is there to compare you anyway.
  • Delayed payouts. If your supplier wants payment now and the platform pays you in a week or more, you are financing the gap yourself.
  • Apps and add-ons. Own-store builders often charge extra for reviews, email tools and shipping integrations.
  • Your time. Photos, listings, packing, customer replies and records take hours every week on either route.

Three sellers, three different answers

SellerSituationSensible first move
AshaSells low-priced accessories. Competes mostly on price. Has Rs 5,000 to start.Marketplace. She needs sales volume and cannot afford ads.
RohanMakes custom gifts that people search for by name. Customers often reorder.Own store plus a marketplace listing. Repeat customers justify the store.
MeeraWants to resell products she has never tested.Neither yet. Order samples and check quality before choosing where to sell.

These are made-up examples, but the logic is real: pick the route that matches your product, your budget and how customers find you, not the one a video told you is best.

A three-question test

  1. Do people already search for this product, or will I have to create the demand?
  2. Can I afford to spend on advertising for a month without any sales?
  3. Will customers want to buy from me again?

If you answered "already search", "no" and "unsure", start on a marketplace. If you answered "I will create demand", "yes" and "yes", an own store becomes more reasonable.

Where to verify this

Rules, fees and menus change. Check the latest version at:

  • The current seller fee pages of each marketplace you are considering.
  • The current pricing page of your store platform, including payment gateway fees.
