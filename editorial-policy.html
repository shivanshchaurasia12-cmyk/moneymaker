Editorial Policy

Our aim is to publish clear, useful and honest educational content for beginners.

Original work

We write our own explanations, examples and calculations. We do not copy or scrape other websites. When we use outside information, we use it for research and then explain it in our own structure and words.

Use of AI tools

We may use AI tools to help with outlining, drafting and checking grammar. A person reads, edits and decides on every article before it is published. These tools do not replace that review.

Examples and numbers

Worked examples are labelled as examples. They use round, simple figures so the idea is easy to follow. They are not current market data, quotes or promises of results.

Rules, taxes and fees

Laws, tax rules, platform fees and menus change. Where an article touches these, it points to the official source so you can check the current version. Treat our article as a guide to the questions to ask, not as the final word.

Corrections

If you tell us about an error, we check it and correct it as soon as we can. Email us through the contact page.

Money and independence

Advertising may appear on this website. Advertisers do not choose, pay for or influence what we write. We do not accept payment to praise a product. If we ever add affiliate links or sponsored content, we will label them clearly on the page.
